Flow Globe manual

Everything the visual does, in the order you meet it.

This manual describes Glyphein Flow Globe, version 1.29. It is written for report authors — the people who place fields in the wells and set the visual up — and most chapters are also useful to the people who read the report.

First edition. It is published ahead of the Microsoft AppSource release, and it grows with the questions people ask. If something is missing or unclear, write to support@glyphein.eu.

  • Getting started

    Add the visual, draw a first map, find your way around the controls.

  • Your data

    What goes in each field well, how rows become flows, and the limits.

  • The views

    Value, Variance to objective, What changed, and your own measures and categories.

  • Map and cards

    Globe and flat map, zoom levels, bundling, filters, cards, selection.

  • Place names

    How names become positions, and what to do when a place is not found.

  • Settings

    The Options panel, the Format pane, languages and accessibility.

  • Notices and diagnostics

    What each notice means, the Diagnostics panel, and common questions.
