Notices and diagnostics

What each notice means, how to read the Diagnostics panel, and answers to common questions.

Flow Globe never drops data without saying so. A notice appears at the top left of the visual; click the panel to read it in full, with the rows concerned and what to do.

Notices

NoticeMeaning and remedy
Add Origin, Destination and a Value to draw flows.A required field is missing.
Add a Value measure to size the flows.Each distinct combination of the fields counts as one until a Value is added.
N unrecognised placesThese names were not found. Use a country, city, port or ISO code, or coordinates. See Place names.
N shared place namesSeveral real places share these names; the largest was used. Add the country or the coordinates to pick another.
N places outside the given country or regionThe country column contradicts the place found. Correct it, or give coordinates.
Render limit reachedMore flows than the render limit. The largest are drawn. Raise the limit in the Format pane, or filter the report.
Row cap reachedThe data is larger than what Power BI sends to the visual. Filter the report or remove unused columns from the wells.
N rows have a Value that is not a numberCounted as 0. Usually a division by zero in the measure.
N rows have no origin or no destinationLeft out. Fill in both ends, or give their coordinates.
N rows with unusable coordinatesOut of range, or not numbers. Use decimal degrees within ±90 / ±180.
N routes have an objective on only part of their rowsTreated as having no objective. Give an objective to every row of those routes, or to none.
Every row goes from a place to itselfOrigin and Destination probably hold the same field. Check the wells.
At the “Countries” level, every flow stays inside a single placeAll flows are internal at this level. Choose a finer level in Group by, or switch Display mode to Auto.
No flow matches the current filters.Minimum value is above every flow. Lower it.
Some views need a licence — showing values instead. No data is hidden.Shown to readers whose licence does not cover the Premium views. The report falls back to the Value view.

The Diagnostics panel

Open Options and choose Diagnostics. The panel shows the version of the visual and counts what happened to your data, line by line:

LineWhat it counts
Input rowsRows received from Power BI, with those merged into a single flow and those going from a place to itself.
Valid routes / Rendered routesFlows found, and flows drawn.
Hidden (render limit) / FilteredFlows left out by the render limit, and by your filters.
Share of volume shownThe part of the total the map is showing.
Unresolved placesNames not found, listed.
Outside the given country or regionPlaces that contradict their country column.
Rows with no origin or no destinationRows left out.
Reciprocal flows · Bundling · Unique categoriesHow many of each.
Cross-highlight · SelectionWhat the report sent to the visual, and what the visual sent back.

When you write to support, the version and a screenshot of this panel are the most useful things you can send.

Common questions

A flow I expect is not on the map.
Check, in this order: a notice about unrecognised places; Routes shown, Minimum value and Cover % of volume in Options; the render limit; and the level of detail — two cities of the same country make no arc at country level.
The numbers are larger than in my table.
A measure that should be averaged — a stock, a rate — is being summed. Set its aggregation to Average in the well. See Your data.
A view button is greyed.
Its field is missing: hover the button to read which one. For a reader, it may also be a view their licence does not include.
The map reframes when I click.
Turn on Lock view in Options, or turn off Fit on selection in the Format pane card Camera.
A place is on the wrong continent.
Its name is shared by several places. Add the country column, or write City, Country.
Can I use it in Report Server, Publish to Web or Embedded?
Write to us before you deploy: these environments behave differently, and we would rather tell you what to expect. See the support page.
