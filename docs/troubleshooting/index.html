Notices and diagnostics

What each notice means, how to read the Diagnostics panel, and answers to common questions.

When Flow Globe sets data aside, it says so. A notice appears at the top left of the visual; click the panel to read it in full, with the rows concerned and what to do.

Notices

NoticeMeaning and remedy
Add Origin, Destination and a Value to draw flows.Origin or Destination is missing.
Add a Value measure to size the flows.Each distinct combination of the fields counts as one until a Value is added.
N unrecognised placesThese names were not found. Use a country, city, port or ISO code, or coordinates. See Place names.
N shared place namesSeveral real places share these names; the largest was used. Add the country or the coordinates to pick another.
N places outside the given country or regionThe country or region given — in the country column or after a comma — does not contain the place found. Correct it, or give coordinates.
Render limit reachedMore flows than the render limit. The largest are drawn. Raise the limit in the Format pane, or filter the report.
Row cap reachedThe visual stopped loading rows while Power BI still had more to send; some flows may be missing. Filter the report, or reduce the number of rows upstream.
N rows have a Value that is not a numberCounted as 0. Usually a division by zero in the measure.
N rows have no origin or no destinationLeft out. Fill in both ends, or give their coordinates.
N rows with unusable coordinatesNot numbers, out of range, only half of the pair, or (0, 0). These ends are placed by name instead. Use decimal degrees within ±90 / ±180, and leave missing cells empty.
“Origin latitude” has no matching longitudeOnly one coordinate field of a pair is in the wells: it is ignored, and places are located by name. Add the other field, or remove this one.
N routes have an objective on only part of their rowsIn the Variance to objective view: treated as having no objective. Give an objective to every row of those routes, or to none.
Every row goes from a place to itselfOrigin and Destination probably hold the same field. Check the wells.
At the “Countries” level, every flow stays inside a single placeAll flows are internal at this level. Choose a finer level in Group by, or switch Display mode to Auto.
No flow matches the current filters.Minimum value is above every flow. Lower it.
The field of the saved view is no longer availableThe report was saved on a view whose field has been removed: Value is shown instead. Re-select a view, or restore the field in the model.
“Measure”: the type … does not match what the column contains.The Measure type set in the Format pane does not fit the data. The visual applies it anyway; check the setting.
Colour thresholds ignoredThe thresholds of a measure view must increase, and be written without a thousands separator.
N bundled corridors — paths are schematicShared legs are simplified for readability. Endpoints, totals and filters are exact. Select a flow to see its own path.
Bundling applies to the Value view onlyBundling is on, but the current view uses colour for something else. Switch to Value, or turn bundling off.
Too many flows to animate them allThe smallest flows are drawn, but still.
This report uses features that require a licence — they are replaced here by their free equivalents.Shown to readers whose licence does not cover a Premium feature the report uses. The report opens in the Value view; Premium filters and indices are set aside.
This report uses features that require a licence — readers without one will see their free equivalents.Shown to the author, while editing, when the report uses a Premium feature.

The Diagnostics panel

Open Options and choose Diagnostics. The panel shows the version of the visual and counts what happened to your data, line by line:

LineWhat it counts
Input rowsRows received from Power BI; below it, rows merged into a single flow, rows whose two ends fall in one place at the current level, and rows going from a place to itself.
Valid routes / Rendered routesFlows found, and flows drawn.
Hidden (render limit) / FilteredFlows left out by the render limit, and by your filters.
Share of volume shownThe part of the total the map is showing.
Unresolved placesNames not found, listed.
Outside the given country or regionPlaces that contradict their country column.
Rows with no origin or no destinationRows left out.
Cross-highlight · Selection gestureWhat the report sent to the visual, and the last selection the visual sent back, with the host's response time.
Licence phaseWhether the licence check has answered, and whether the report is being read or edited.
Unusable coordinates · Aggregated coordsRows whose coordinates were refused, and places whose coordinates look like placeholders.
Reciprocal flows · Bundling · Unique categoriesHow many of each; the bundling line appears when bundling is on.
RecommendationsAt the bottom, when they apply: render limit reached, high unresolved rate, more than 20 distinct categories.

When you write to support, the version and a screenshot of this panel are the most useful things you can send.

Common questions

A flow I expect is not on the map.
Check, in this order: a notice about unrecognised places; Routes shown, Minimum value and Cover % of volume in Options; the render limit; and the level of detail — two cities of the same country make no arc at country level.
The numbers are larger than in my table.
The visual adds up the rows of a flow — across categories, and across the cities of a country at country level — so a stock or a rate gets summed. Keep an additive measure in Value, and put a rate in Extra measures with Measure type set to Rate or average. See Your data.
A view is greyed in the view menu.
Its field is missing: the menu writes which one under its name. It may also be a view your licence does not include — Not included in your plan.
The map reframes when I click.
Turn on Lock view in Options, or turn off Fit on selection in the Format pane card Camera.
A place is on the wrong continent.
Its name is shared by several places. Add the country column, or write City, Country.
Can I use it in Report Server, Publish to Web or Embedded?
Write to us before you deploy: these environments behave differently, and we would rather tell you what to expect. See the support page.
